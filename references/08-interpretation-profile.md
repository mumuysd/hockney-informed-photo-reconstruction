# Unified Reconstruction and Render Recipe v0.7

## Purpose

Convert one accepted `07` v0.6 reading into one reconstruction thesis and one `render_recipe` v0.4. The recipe is the only pictorial instruction source allowed to reach the prompt compiler.

Do not copy the observation report into Imagegen. Do not build a parallel Visual Plan schema. If the source relation, implementation, preservation core, fidelity locks, profile, carrier candidates, or rewrite opportunity is unsupported, return to `07`.

## Build one reconstruction thesis

Write one sentence naming:

1. the source-present relation that survives;
2. the dominant carrier or structural driver;
3. the visible reorganization that replaces camera organization.

Reject theses led by an artist name, medium, mood adjective, `colorful`, `loose`, `flat`, `painterly`, `expressive`, or generic simplification.

## Build the recipe in decision order

### 1. Lock relation and feasible fidelity

Copy `source_relation`, `relation_implementation`, matching `profile_id`, and the three-part core unchanged. Copy exact user requirements into `fidelity_locks`. Stop if feasibility is `blocked`.

### 2. Build positive shape replacements

Turn the candidate carriers into exactly three to five `shape_map` entries. For every entry state:

- the source-present region;
- the replacement carrier that should be visible first;
- a concrete merge, truncate, widen, lift, stack, interlock, open, or mass-only action;
- what must no longer be individually drawn.

Positive replacement precedes negative inventory. A building field becomes grouped masses with explicitly budgeted non-repeating recognition exemplars; stairs become one governing wedge or route relation; a product becomes named masses and openings; an arrangement becomes named grouped blocks. Do not rely only on a long avoid list, and do not destroy the minimum legibility layer merely to lower the object count.

### 3. Compile spatial rewrite and deletion

Use one structural driver with at least two mutually reinforcing manifestations. Then assign high, medium, and quiet regions and convert the deletion plan into a source-specific `non_drawing_contract`.

Deletion means merge, omit, leave open, or represent only as mass—not blur, reduced contrast, paper texture, or an empty border.

Apply **Localized expression** from `render-profiles.md` inside the existing fields. High regions name the rhythm or expression to retain; medium regions carry supporting fragments; quiet regions remain broad watercolor fields or open ground. Name both suppressed repeated families and useful groups that must survive in `non_drawing_contract`. Place these zones in the reconstructed composition, without freezing source coordinates or deleting character just to reduce mark count.

For `living_subject_in_expansive_scene`, complete `expansive_environment`: one or two environmental carriers inside the same total budget, exactly two or three functional anchor groups, at most one or two non-repeating recognition-critical exemplars per group, non-empty merged inventory, one subordinate setting family, a subject-environment link, and a fields/grouped-place-before-exemplar thumbnail read. Otherwise set it to `not_applicable`.

### 4. Build structural luminosity

Use one continuous in-scene luminous carrier, one smaller dark anchor, one or two high-chroma regions, non-empty medium and restrained tiers, at least one value contrast, at least one warm-cool or complementary contrast, and color-plane shadows without continuous naturalistic modeling.

Color may depart from the source when roles remain coherent. Do not use a fixed palette, uniform saturation, uniform brightening, gray mud, white-margin light, or photographic shadow modeling.

### 5. Rank edge and mark jobs

Assign firm, broken/open, and soft/lost edge roles by region. Create exactly two or three mark families. Each family declares:

```yaml
priority: [primary, secondary, subordinate]
geometry:
region:
excluded_regions: []
job:
```

At least one family is `primary`; priorities cannot all be equal. Primary identity/recognition lines stay exclusive to named junctions. Environment, quiet fields, and broad body/object fields cannot inherit the same fine contour merely because it looks expressive.

Within `geometry` and `job`, specify source-responsive pressure changes, broken turns, and any purposeful local echo. Map expression, depth, or contact to the relevant family without adding a separate drawing layer. Broad color fields must still organize the whole image; supporting facial or hair cues cannot become polished portrait modeling. Respect the existing quiet ceiling and all exclusions.

### 6. Translate into regional watercolor behavior

Choose the profile-matching material mode:

- `transparent_line_and_wash_portrait`;
- `open_wash_and_directional_brush_landscape`;
- `selective_contour_and_wash_object`.

Cover at least one broad field and one precision/recognition/contact/anchor region. Name visible evidence such as wash transparency, clean overlap, pressure change, compact wet deposition, dry interruption, pooling, or exposed ground. Block opaque all-over paint, muddy repeated mixing, global paper grain, generalized blooms, and one repeated treatment everywhere.

### 7. Name one default prior

Name the most likely source-specific fallback, such as fully modeled portrait, descriptive pet texture, real-estate rendering, technical/product illustration, advertising gloss, object-by-object still life, vehicle catalog image, tourist landscape watercolor, or camera-preserving watercolor repaint. This is one concise blocker, not an exhaustive negative catalogue.

## Acceptance gate

Accept only when:

1. source ID, displayed orientation, relation, implementation, user locks, and three-part core are unchanged from `07`;
2. profile and material mode match the relation;
3. exact fidelity feasibility is not blocked;
4. one thesis coordinates every field;
5. shape map contains three to five positive replacements with forbidden individualization;
6. one driver has at least two visible manifestations;
7. information tiers differ and quiet ceiling is categorical;
8. non-drawing names real source inventory and protects the core;
9. expansive environment is complete only when applicable;
10. structural luminosity has complete carrier, anchor, hierarchy, contrasts, and shadow policy;
11. two or three marks have unequal priorities, geometrically distinct jobs, and explicit excluded regions; their local rhythm retains useful expression, depth, or contact without restoring repeated inventory or displacing watercolor-field dominance;
12. material mode covers broad and precision/recognition regions with observable evidence;
13. material applications do not create undeclared marks or quiet-region violations;
14. one default prior is blocked;
15. no artist name, external visual Skill, reference artwork, poster default, fixed blank percentage, typography, print identity, vague render language, duplicated schema, or unresolved decision remains.
